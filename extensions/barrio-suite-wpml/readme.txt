Languages of the Barrio Suite forms from WPML.

== Description ==

With WPML active, the Barrio Suite builder shows a language switcher: every text of a form, of its notifications and of its integrations can be translated. Forms and emails are shown in the visitor's language and entries keep it. Without WPML the extension does nothing; translations already saved stay in the database.

It needs Barrio Suite.
